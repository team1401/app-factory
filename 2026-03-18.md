## Winner: LoadOrder (#1)

**Why:** The GM problem is one of the most concrete, recurring pain points in tabletop gaming. Every session, every GM hits it. Roll20 and D&D Beyond solve it only if you've pre-built your whole campaign in their ecosystem. For one-shot sessions, convention games, or new GMs, you need something that works in 30 seconds with zero setup. LoadOrder is that tool.

**GateMate test:** ✅ Any GM immediately says "YES, I have died on a napkin in the middle of a boss fight because I forgot who had Stunned on them."

**Strong second:** PasteDiff — would have been a clean build but slightly more developer-niche with existing competition (diffchecker.com). LoadOrder has a clearer niche with less polished competition.

## App Built: LoadOrder

- **URL:** http://100.97.65.28:3165
- **Port:** 3165 (airstream)
- **Container:** loadorder
- **Style:** "Letterpress Broadside" — 19th century theatrical print aesthetic. Warm cream paper, navy & crimson ink, Ultra woodtype serif font. Completely unexpected for a fantasy combat tool — the contrast between stately Victorian typography and "the dragon has 4 more death save failures" is the whole vibe.
- **Features:**
  - Auto-sorted initiative order (descending)
  - Per-combatant HP bar with color-shift (sage → copper → crimson as HP drains)
  - Damage / Heal / Temp HP controls
  - All 14 D&D 5e conditions as toggle chips
  - Concentration indicator
  - Death saves (3-pip tracker for successes/failures)
  - Round counter with elapsed game-time (6s per round)
  - Per-combatant notes field
  - localStorage persistence (survives page refresh)
  - Keyboard shortcuts: Space/→ = Next Turn, ← = Prev, N = focus add form
  - Empty states, error states, flash animations on HP changes
  - Responsive (mobile-first considerations)
- **Revenue path:** Free (all features), Paid ($3/mo) = saved encounter library + monster compendium lookup + encounter sharing links
