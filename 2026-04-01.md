## Brainstorm (Problem-First)

1. **KeyChart — Chord Chart Transposer** ← SELECTED
   - Pain: Musicians need to transpose chord charts to different keys for different singers/instruments. Existing tools require finding the song on their site or are buried in complex apps. No clean paste-and-go solution exists.
   - Who: Working musicians, worship teams, cover bands, teachers, singer-songwriters
   - Frequency: Every rehearsal, every gig — weekly at minimum
   - Existing gaps: Ultimate Guitar only works for their songs. Other tools require registration or barely work.

   - Pain: Sound engineers need to coordinate wireless mic frequencies to avoid IM distortion

3. **CheckFront — Retail Receiving Sheet**

4. **CertTrack — Professional Certification Expiry Tracker**

5. **ClinicBoard — Small Clinic Room Status Whiteboard**
   - Pain: Dental/chiro/PT offices use physical whiteboards for room status, causing miscommunication
   - Rejected: Needs real-time multi-user sync — too complex for Tier 1

6. **TimeFence — Deep Work Session Blocker with Commitment Contract**
   - Pain: Remote workers need hard focus time blocked, not just reminders
   - Rejected: Overlaps with browser extension space, not distinctive enough

## Why KeyChart Won

KeyChart solves a specific, recurring frustration for a massive audience (any musician who plays with other people). The problem is universally understandable: "I have a chord chart in G but my singer needs it in A." Existing solutions either require the song to be on their platform (Ultimate Guitar), or are buried in guitar tab apps. A clean, frictionless paste-and-transpose tool fills a clear gap. The compass rose Circle of Fifths adds an interactive element that teaches music theory while you work. Clear freemium path: free basic → saved library, band sharing, mobile app.

## Build Details

- **App:** KeyChart — Chord Chart Transposer
- **Port:** 3191
- **URL:** http://100.97.65.28:3191
- **Style:** "Admiralty Chart / Nautical Pilot" — warm parchment, navy ink, Cinzel headers, compass rose Circle of Fifths
- **Stack:** Pure HTML/CSS/JS single file, nginx, Docker
- **Features:**
  - Interactive Circle of Fifths compass rose for target key selection
  - Auto-detect source key from chart content
  - Full transposition with smart chord regex (handles maj7, m7b5, dim7, sus4, slash chords)
  - Sharp/flat preference auto-set per key, manually overridable
  - Capo suggestions for guitarists (top 3 options)
  - 3 example presets (hymn, jazz, pop)
  - Copy to clipboard
  - Responsive layout
