### Trend Inspiration
"AI slop" is a huge viral topic right now — the term for AI-generated text that floods the internet with bland, formulaic prose. Multiple viral articles and tools this week focused on detecting and critiquing it. Perfect for a self-contained, no-backend web app.

### What It Does
Paste any text and receive a full tabloid-newspaper front-page exposé revealing its "AI fingerprints." Seven forensic metrics scored from 0–100:

1. **AI Buzzword Density** — 50+ overused AI phrases ("certainly," "it's important to note," etc.)
2. **Hedge Phrase Infestation** — epistemic cowardice language
3. **Transition Word Abuse** — "Furthermore," "Moreover," "In conclusion" overuse
4. **Passive Voice Evasion** — heuristic passive voice detection
5. **Sentence Length Robotics** — low variance = robotic cadence
6. **Sycophantic Openers** — "Certainly!" "Great question!" sentence starters
7. **Suspicious Em Dash Usage** — AI's favorite punctuation

Results are displayed as a 1980s tabloid front page with:
- Sensational headline based on SLOP SCORE
- Three investigative column articles with evidence lists
- Circular SLOP SCORE seal (0–100)
- Bottom analysis + scorecard sidebar
- Rubber stamp overlay (EXPOSED / CLEARED / SUSPICIOUS / AUTHENTIC)
- Pull quote from the most suspicious sentence
- Pre-loaded examples for "AI text" vs "Human text"

### Visual Style
**Retro Tabloid Sensationalism** — Weekly World News / National Enquirer 1980s aesthetic.

- **Palette:** Newsprint cream (#F2ECD9), ink black (#1A1A14), scandal red (#B8160A), dateline gold (#A07820)
- **Typography:** Playfair Display (headlines) + IM Fell English (body columns) + Special Elite (datelines, stamps, prices)
- **Signature:** Dense justified column layout, double-rule borders, rubber stamp overlays at rotate(-8deg), aged paper texture via repeating linear-gradient, evidence boxes with dotted rules
- **NOT glassmorphism** ✅
