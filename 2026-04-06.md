# App Factory Ship Log — 2026-04-06

## Brainstorm (problem-first)

1) **Redaction Runner** — *HR managers, journalists, researchers, lawyers* regularly need to share transcripts/notes but must remove PII; doing it manually in Word/Docs is error-prone.
2) **Planogram Punchlist** — *Retail merchandisers / store managers* must audit shelf/fixture compliance weekly; current tools are heavyweight enterprise apps or paper checklists.
3) **Union Call-Time Confirmer** — *Film/TV ADs + department heads* juggle daily call sheets and confirmations; chasing acknowledgements is constant friction (especially last-minute changes).
4) **Kitchen Allergen Matrix Builder** — *Small restaurants* need a clean allergen cross-contact matrix for staff; most use messy spreadsheets or outdated binder pages.
5) **Teacher Accommodation Quick-Sheets** — *Special ed coordinators / teachers* need a fast way to produce “student-specific accommodation snapshots” for substitute folders without leaking sensitive data.
6) **Lab Sample Label Sanity Check** — *Small wet labs* regularly print labels; one swapped ID ruins days. They need a lightweight pre-print validation checklist and “label batch audit” view.

## Picked idea: Redaction Runner

**Why this won:** It’s a real, recurring pain with real consequences (accidental PII leaks). Existing solutions are either expensive enterprise suites, clunky PDF tooling, or manual search/replace. A local-first “redaction desk” people can keep bookmarked and use weekly hits the GateMate bar: specific user, specific frustration, clear value.

## App shipped

- **Name:** Redaction Runner
- **Target user:** HR/People Ops, journalists, researchers, legal ops — anyone sharing transcripts/incident notes
- **Pain point:** “I need to share this text, but I’m terrified I’ll miss a phone/email/SSN and leak PII.”
- **Core workflow:** Paste → detect & highlight → replace → export redacted text + JSON report
- **Design:** “Library Card Catalog Privacy Desk” (tab-notched catalog cards + CONFIDENTIAL stamp)
