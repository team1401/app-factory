## CueCard

**App Name:** CueCard  
**Tagline:** Speaker cue cards that actually work  
**URL:** http://100.97.65.28:3163  
**Style:** "Warm Stage Light"

### Description
CueCard is a browser-based speaker preparation tool that turns talk outlines into printable cue cards with production-level detail. Build your talk slide-by-slide, add cue annotations (GO, PAUSE, ENGAGE, NOTE, WARN), set per-slide timing, and get color-coded cue cards ready to print or present from the built-in full-screen teleprompter mode.

### Pain Point
Public speakers — from conference presenters to wedding toasters — rely on either bare PowerPoint notes (which disappear from view during presentation) or handwritten index cards (which are fragile and disorganized). There's no tool that bridges structured speaker notes with the production cue system professional stage managers use. Speakers miss timing cues, forget transitions, and don't know how to flag moments requiring audience engagement vs. a pause vs. a GO signal.

### Target User
Conference speakers, TED-style presenters, workshop facilitators, teachers, wedding toasters, and anyone who gives a structured talk more than once. Especially useful for speakers who want to think like a stage director, not just a slide deck creator.

### Revenue Potential
- Freemium: free for basic use, $5–9/month for PDF export, multiple saved talks, team sharing
- Conference circuit niche: strong word-of-mouth among speakers who present regularly
- Coaching tie-in: speaking coaches could white-label or recommend as part of their toolkit
- Solid product-led growth potential if "print your cue cards" is a shareable output
