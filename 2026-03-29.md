# App Factory Build — 2026-03-29

## Brainstorm (Problem-First)

1. **SDS Station** — OSHA requires Safety Data Sheets instantly accessible during inspections/emergencies. Small workshops (auto shops, maker spaces, school labs) either pay for enterprise EHS suites or use messy paper binders/shared drives. Pain frequency: every inspection, every emergency, every new chemical arrival. Existing solutions miss: simplicity, local-first, no auth needed, kiosk mode for wall tablet.

2. **Seed Vault** — Home gardeners lose track of what seeds they have, planting dates, and germination rates. Pain: buying duplicates, missing planting windows. Existing solutions are either full farm management suites or basic spreadsheets.

3. **Tool Checkout** — Shared workshops/makerspaces can't track who borrowed what tool. Pain: tools go missing, no accountability. Existing: enterprise asset management, overkill.

4. **Batch Label** — Small-batch food/cosmetic producers need compliant ingredient labels. Pain: FDA/labeling requirements are complex, current tools are enterprise or expensive. Frequency: every new product batch.

5. **Permit Tracker** — Small contractors juggle multiple active permits across jurisdictions. Pain: missed inspections, expired permits, no single view. Existing: spreadsheets or expensive construction management suites.

6. **Calibration Log** — Shops with precision instruments need calibration tracking for ISO/quality audits. Pain: paper logs get lost, auditors need instant proof. Frequency: monthly-quarterly per instrument.

## Selection: SDS Station

**Why it won:** Highest combination of pain severity (OSHA fines, emergency access), frequency (daily shop operations), clear target user (any small shop owner), and gap in market (no simple local-first solution exists between "paper binder" and "$10K/yr enterprise EHS"). The one-sentence test passes immediately: "I need to find the SDS for acetone RIGHT NOW because an inspector just walked in." Universal across industries — auto shops, school chem labs, maker spaces, art studios, any workshop with chemicals.
