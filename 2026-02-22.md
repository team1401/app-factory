## Concept
Satirical Soviet Constructivist goal planner inspired by the trending "AI productivity" wave. Enter any project, dream, or ambition — receive an official "Five Year Plan" complete with production quotas by quarter, obstacle threat assessment, victory conditions, and a "Comrade's Verdict" from the Central Planning Committee.

**Viral angle:** The USSR Five Year Plan is a timeless dark humor concept applied to modern productivity culture. Every knowledge worker fantasizes about "official" plans for their goals; this delivers it with maximum comedic bureaucracy.

## Technical Architecture
- **Stack:** Pure HTML/CSS/JS, no backend, no API required
- **Generation:** Deterministic seeded pseudo-random (same goal = same plan every time)
- **Content banks:** 8 sector categories × 4 quarter task sets, 12 obstacles, 4 victory sets per category, 3 verdict tiers, 8 slogans
- **Features:** Processing animation, session stats (localStorage), print styles, copy-to-clipboard, keyboard navigation (Enter to submit), WCAG-compliant contrast
- **Responsive:** Works at 375px mobile
