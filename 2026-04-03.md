# App Factory Build — 2026-04-03

## Brainstorm (Problem-First)

1. **SafeStock — Inventory Reorder Point Calculator** ← SELECTED
   - Pain: Small e-commerce sellers (Etsy, Amazon FBA, Shopify) either run out of stock (lost sales) or over-order (tied-up capital). Enterprise ERP is $$$. Most sellers use gut instinct.
   - Who: ~2.5M active Etsy sellers, ~2M Amazon FBA sellers, millions of small retailers
   - Frequency: Weekly — every restock decision
   - Existing gaps: Enterprise ERP (SAP, NetSuite) costs $10K+/yr. No clean, free, instant calculator exists that handles safety stock, reorder points, AND EOQ together with visualization.
   - Revenue path: Free tier (10 SKUs), Pro ($9/mo for unlimited + demand forecasting + supplier lead time tracking)

2. **DirtWork — Soil Amendment Calculator**
   - Pain: "My soil test says pH 5.8, what do I add?" — gardeners struggle with amendment math
   - Who: 77M US gardening households
   - Rejected: Seasonal use (spring only), less recurring than SafeStock

3. **DyeLot — Yarn Stash Inventory for Knitters**
   - Pain: Buying duplicate yarn, not having enough mid-project
   - Who: 38M US knitters/crafters
   - Rejected: Ravelry exists (controversial but functional), and this is inventory-tracking which gets close to previous builds

4. **LyeCalc — Cold Process Soap Calculator**
   - Pain: Lye-to-oil ratios are chemistry; wrong amounts are dangerous
   - Who: ~2M handmade soap makers
   - Rejected: SoapCalc.net exists and despite awful UI, is deeply entrenched. Hard to displace.

5. **HalfLife — Supplement Pharmacokinetics Tracker**
   - Pain: "Is my caffeine still active?" — people don't know drug half-lives
   - Who: Supplement users, medication managers
   - Rejected: Medical liability concerns, variable individual metabolism makes outputs unreliable

6. **StudioRate — Photography Day Rate Builder**
   - Pain: Freelance creatives undercharge because they don't account for all costs
   - Who: Freelance photographers/videographers
   - Rejected: Too close to TrueHour (already built a rate calculator)

7. **PrepScale — Restaurant Prep Scaling Calculator**
   - Pain: Over-prepping (waste) or under-prepping (86'd items)
   - Who: Kitchen managers
   - Rejected: Too close to mise (already built a kitchen timing tool)

## Why SafeStock Won

SafeStock hits the GateMate standard: specific pain point (inventory reorder timing), identifiable users (small e-commerce sellers), recurring need (weekly), no beautiful existing solution. The math is well-defined (safety stock formula, Wilson EOQ, reorder point calculation), the visualization opportunities are rich (sparklines, timelines, status indicators), and the Product Hunt angle writes itself: "Stop guessing when to reorder — SafeStock does the math."

## Design Direction

**Style: "Tufte Data Ink"** — Edward Tufte's information design principles. Maximum data, minimum decoration. Warm cream paper (#FEFCF8), Source Serif 4 for body text, IBM Plex Mono for computed values, single accent blue (#2563EB) for key metrics. The data IS the design — no chart junk, no gratuitous gradients.

**Signature element:** Inline SVG sparklines in every table row showing 30-day stock depletion projections with dashed reorder-point lines and safety-stock boundaries — Tufte's "small multiples" concept.

## Architecture

- Single HTML file with embedded CSS/JS (Tier 1)
- No backend — all math runs client-side
- localStorage persistence for SKU data and settings
- Core formulas: Safety Stock (Z × σd × √L), Reorder Point (d̄ × L + SS), EOQ (Wilson: √(2DS/H))
- Export to CSV and JSON
