## What I Built

**Back to 2016** - A nostalgia-driven time capsule generator that recreates your 2016 self

## Why This App

Tapped into the massively viral "2016 is the new 2016" trend currently dominating TikTok and social media. According to recent data:
- 55M+ TikTok videos using 2016-style filters
- #BringBack2016 trending across platforms
- Spotify playlists titled "2016" seeing major playback increases
- Represents nostalgia for pre-pandemic, pre-AI times when "the internet was still fun"

This trend is a perfect match for the App Factory mission - timely, achievable in one session, and genuinely viral.

## Features

- **Personalized memories** based on age (you were X years old in 2016, here's what you were probably doing)
- **2016 music hits** - Drake, Chainsmokers, Rihanna curated by user's vibe
- **Viral moments** - Mannequin Challenge, Pokémon GO launch, Damn Daniel, Harambe, Bottle Flip
- **Popular apps of 2016** - Vine, Dubsmash, Musical.ly nostalgia
- **Slang translator** - "on fleek," "lit," "bae," "savage" with context
- **No backend required** - all content curated/hardcoded for speed and reliability

## Visual Design

**Style Direction:** Vintage Digital Scrapbook

Breaking completely from the dark glassmorphism plague. Instead, channeled authentic mid-2010s internet aesthetics:

**Color Palette:**
- Hot pink (#FF6B9D) - millennial pink era
- Instagram blue (#4A90E2) - 2016 Instagram's signature color
- Snapchat yellow (#FFD93D)
- Warm cream background (#FFF8E7) - paper/scrapbook texture
- Warm dark gray text (#2C3E50) - not harsh black

**Typography:**
- Montserrat Bold for headers (very 2016)
- Open Sans for body (Google's darling from that era)
- Generous, friendly sizing

**Signature Element:**
Polaroid cards that "develop" on hover - they start faded/washed out and gain color/contrast, mimicking real Polaroid photo development. Each card has subtle random rotation (-2deg to +2deg) like they're scattered on a cork board.

**Layout:**
CSS Grid with scattered placement, paper texture background, polaroid photo frames with generous white borders and handwritten-style captions underneath.

## Build Notes

**Challenge:** Initial react-scripts build had dependency conflicts (ajv-keywords issue). Solved by switching to CDN-based React setup - simpler, faster, more reliable for this use case.

**Port:** 3110 (3100-3109 were occupied by other Factory apps)

**Deploy method:** Plain Docker instead of docker-compose (compose had ContainerConfig bug with the existing image cache)
