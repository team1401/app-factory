## Brainstorm: Problem-First Ideas

### 1. SlateBoard — Woodworker Cut Optimizer
**Pain:** Woodworkers waste expensive plywood ($80–150/sheet) due to bad cutting plans. Planning cuts manually or in Excel is tedious and inefficient.  
**Who:** 5M+ hobbyist woodworkers (r/woodworking), professional cabinetmakers, DIY furniture builders  
**Frequency:** Every project (anyone doing any cabinet, furniture, or shelving build)  
**Existing:** CutList Plus ($89 one-time, desktop-only, Windows). No good free web tool.  
**One-sentence test:** "I spent $200 on plywood and had way too much waste because I didn't plan my cuts." ✅

## Selection: SlateBoard

**Why this one:**
1. **Strongest problem/solution fit** — the pain is measurable in dollars ($80–150/wasted sheet)
2. **Zero good free web alternative** — CutList Plus is the only real competitor at $89, desktop-only
3. **Algorithmically interesting** — produces a visual output (SVG cut diagrams) that's inherently satisfying
4. **Brian is a woodworker** (mentioned in USER.md — Cannondale, Traeger, woodworking)  
5. **Huge community** — r/woodworking has 5M+ subscribers, constant "how do I plan my cuts?" posts  
6. **Clear freemium path** — free for 2 sheets, paid for unlimited + PDF export + grain-direction locking  
7. **Not in the style log** — "American Hardware Catalog" style is completely fresh

## Technical Decisions

- **Algorithm:** Best-Fit Decreasing Height (BFDH) shelf packing  
  - Sort pieces by area descending (largest first)
  - For each piece, prefer landscape orientation (shorter dimension as height)  
  - Find existing shelf with minimum height gap where piece fits
  - If no shelf fits, open new shelf at bottom of sheet
  - If no sheet has room, open new sheet
  - Oversized pieces flagged with warning, not silently dropped
- **Rotation support:** Each part has "Allow 90° rotation" toggle. Prefers landscape (shorter height)
- **Rendering:** SVG-based cut diagrams, generated client-side. Each sheet rendered with:
  - Plywood-tone background
  - Diagonal hatch for unused waste areas  
  - 8 distinct dark-desaturated part colors
  - Sequence number badges (circled orange)
  - Steel-blue dimension annotations outside the sheet frame
  - Piece label + dimensions inside each rect
- **Animation:** Pieces "stamp" onto the sheet with staggered 120ms delays (scale+opacity keyframe)
- **Tech stack:** Pure HTML/CSS/JS (no framework), nginx, Docker

## Visual Style: "American Hardware Catalog"

- Background: Aged paper (#F5EFDF) — warm, like actual catalog pages
- Surface: Pure white cards with warm borders
- Primary: Hardware orange-red (#C94A0B) — True Value / Ace Hardware vibe
- Dimension lines: Steel blue (#3D5A8A)
- Typography: Oswald 700 (headers/labels) + IBM Plex Mono (measurements) + Inter (body)
- Sheet material: Plywood tone (#C8A86A) with diagonal hatch for waste
- Part colors: 8 dark-desaturated colors (Prussian blue, Forest green, Brick red, Deep purple, Dark teal, Sienna, Slate, Olive)
- Signature: SVG cut diagrams as catalog technical illustrations with engineering-style dimension annotations
