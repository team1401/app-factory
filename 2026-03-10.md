## Why BakerMath Won

**Problem statement**: In baking, ALL percentages are relative to flour weight (100%), not total dough. This is called "baker's percentage" or "baker's math." To scale a recipe, you can't just multiply — you need to solve for flour first, then every other ingredient relative to that. Most bakers do this wrong or need a spreadsheet.

**"Oh yeah, that's annoying" test**: ✅ Any baker who's tried to halve a recipe for sourdough has hit this exact wall.

**Community size**: The sourdough community exploded post-2020 and is enormous. Home baking is a committed hobby with passionate practitioners who invest in good tools.

**Existing solutions**: Spreadsheets, paper, nothing else. No polished dedicated web tool exists.

**Revenue path**: Free calculator → saved recipe vault (freemium) → bakery business tier (cost-per-loaf, team formula library)

**Key differentiator vs. GateMate standard**: GateMate solves a logistical coordination problem. BakerMath solves a math/scaling problem that requires ongoing use — every time you bake something different or want to scale up.

## Features Shipped

- **Dual input modes**: Flour weight → calculate grams; OR Total dough weight → solve for flour first
- **Dynamic ingredient table**: Add/remove ingredients, edit names and percentages, real-time gram calculation
- **Multi-flour support**: Multiple flour ingredients tracked properly (e.g., 80% bread flour + 20% whole wheat)
- **Loaves multiplier**: Scale 1–20 loaves with a stepper
- **7 preset formulas**: Sourdough Sandwich, Sourdough Crusty, French Baguette, Neapolitan Pizza, Focaccia, Ciabatta, NY Bagel
- **Ensō hydration ring**: Visual circle gauge with 6 descriptor levels + example bread types
- **Baker's contextual notes**: Smart tips about salt ratio, starter percentage, high-hydration technique
- **Copy Recipe Card**: Formatted plaintext formula with all percentages and gram amounts
- **Print support**: Clean print layout
- **Responsive**: Works on mobile (375px) and desktop
