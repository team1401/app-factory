# App Factory — 2026-02-28

## Brainstorm

1. **Post Mortem** — AI forensic autopsy reports for failed projects, habits, and ideas. Styled as a County Medical Examiner form with cause of death, toxicology, timeline of decline, and a pathologist's final assessment. Darkly funny, cathartic, deeply shareable.

2. **Patent Pending** — Generate fake-but-convincing USPTO patent applications for ridiculous ideas. Numbered claims, abstract, prior art references. Comedy + weirdly useful for ideation.

3. **The Dossier** — CIA/FBI intelligence dossier generator for fictional characters. For D&D game masters and fiction writers.

4. **Wires Crossed** — Paste an argument transcript; AI identifies exactly where the miscommunication happened. Diagrams as a circuit board with broken connections.

5. **Dead Reckoning** — Life navigation chart tool. Enter your current position in key life areas and goals; get a nautical chart showing your trajectory to each destination with hazards and course corrections.

6. **Chronotype Clock** — Enter sleep patterns and alert windows; get a personalized 24-hour biological optimization schedule overlaid on a clock visualization.

7. **Noise Composer** — Turn any dataset (stock prices, weather, seismic readings) into ambient generative music. Data sonification as art.

## Winner: Post Mortem

**Why:** It won on every axis.

- **Surprise factor:** Nobody has built this. "Autopsy report for my failed podcast" is a sentence that makes you stop and laugh, then immediately want to try it.
- **Universal appeal:** Every person on earth has a dead project. This isn't an RV tool, not a tech tool — it's a *human* tool.
- **Viral potential:** Sky-high. "Post Mortem of My Startup" is a natural Product Hunt/Hacker News headline. People will screenshot the results and share them.
- **Emotional utility:** Weirdly therapeutic. Getting clinical closure on a dead project ("Official Cause of Death: Chronic Scope Creep Syndrome, Critical") is both funny and genuinely helpful.
- **Visual style:** County Medical Examiner report — Courier Prime monospace, teal section headers, red stamps, numbered form sections — is unlike anything in the style log and looks legitimately official.
- **AI integration:** The AI fills in the clinical language beautifully. "Toxicology Findings: Acute Perfectionism — Critical. Chronic Scope Creep — Elevated." This is exactly the kind of AI-powered humor that earns the CPU cycles.

## Build Details

- **Port:** 3155 (airstream)
- **Stack:** Node.js/Express backend, Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS frontend, Ollama (qwen2.5:14b) + llama.cpp proxy fallback

  - AI-generated vs template badge indicator
  - Print button
  - "File Another Case" reset
  - Full fallback template if AI unavailable
