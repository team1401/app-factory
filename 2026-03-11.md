## Why MeetCost Won

The "one-sentence test" passes instantly: *"You have no idea your weekly team standup costs $180,000 a year in salary time."*

Differentiators vs existing tools (Doodle widget, 2016 HBR calculator):
- **Recurrence engine**: Annual + 5-year cost projections — not just a one-time number
- **Opportunity cost framing**: What could that budget fund instead? (contractor days, AWS compute, conference tickets, team lunches, books, SaaS tools)
- **Role-tier salary presets**: 9 presets so you don't need exact salaries
- **Utility bill format**: The invoice framing is shareable — "I'm sending my team their meeting invoice" is an actual conversation starter
- **Tear-off remittance stub**: Authenticated the utility bill aesthetic with a genuine signature element

## Technical Architecture

**Type:** Static frontend (pure HTML/CSS/JS) — no backend, no API calls  
**Delivery:** Nginx 1.27-alpine in Docker, host networking (bridge pool exhausted on airstream)  
**Calculation engine:**
- `hourlyRate(salary) = salary / 2080`
- `meetingCost = sum(hourlyRate(a) × durationHours)` for all attendees
- `annualCost = meetingCost × meetingsPerWeek × weeksPerYear`
- `5yearCost = annualCost × 5`

**State:** Vanilla JS, no framework. Attendees array + meeting config. All reactive — invoice re-renders on every input change.
