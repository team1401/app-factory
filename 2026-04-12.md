# App Factory Brainstorm — 2026-04-12

## Brainstorm List

### 1. MedHuddle
- **Pain:** Medical residents rotate across hospitals monthly and must manually reconstruct case logs, procedure counts, and competency milestones for ACGME accreditation. This is tedious, error-prone, and usually neglected until deadline panic.
- **Target:** Medical residents and fellows (all specialties), ~140,000 in the US alone
- **Frequency:** Daily logging needed; most do it weekly-at-best, then cram before semi-annual reviews
- **Existing solutions:** MedHub (enterprise, hospital-purchased, clunky), New Innovations (institutional, expensive), paper logs. None are resident-first or mobile-friendly in a way that feels modern.
- **Why they fall short:** Institutional tools are designed for program directors, not residents. They're slow, require VPN access, and feel like EHRs. Residents want something fast and personal.
- **Visual style:** Medical Chart Clipboard — warm clinical paper stock, ruled lines, stamped chart headers, Caveat handwritten labels, IBM Plex Mono data, teal clinical accents
- **Freemium path:** Free tier for personal case logging (up to 200 entries); paid tier for ACGME report generation, specialty-specific milestone tracking, and export to MedHub/New Innovations formats

### 2. PartFix
- **Pain:** Auto mechanics waste 20+ minutes per repair searching cross-reference catalogs, dealer portals, and aftermarket databases to find the right part number for a specific VIN
- **Target:** Independent auto repair shop technicians (~750K in US)
- **Frequency:** Multiple times daily
- **Existing solutions:** RockAuto (web catalog), PartsTech (shop management integration), dealer portals. All require VIN decode → navigate menus → cross-reference.
- **Why they fall short:** Too many clicks, no unified search across OEM+aftermarket, no mobile-optimized quick-lookup
- **Visual style:** Workshop Pegboard — dark pegboard background, metal shelf labels, orange accent hardware tags

### 3. TuningFork
- **Pain:** Private music teachers (piano, violin, guitar, voice) teaching 30-50 weekly students have no lightweight tool for lesson notes, repertoire tracking, and student progression. Spreadsheets and paper notebooks don't scale.
- **Target:** Private music instructors (~300K in US)
- **Frequency:** After every lesson (daily)
- **Existing solutions:** Music Teacher's Helper (dated, $), plain notebooks, Google Sheets
- **Why they fall short:** MTH is stuck in 2015 UI-wise; most teachers just use notebooks because no app is fast enough to log during/between lessons
- **Visual style:** Sheet Music Staff — cream paper background, staff line dividers, musical notation accents

### 4. PlotWeed
- **Pain:** Community garden coordinators manage 50-200 plots with paper maps, WhatsApp groups, and spreadsheets. Seasonal turnover, waitlists, and maintenance tracking is chaos.
- **Target:** Community garden managers/coordinators (~40K gardens in US)
- **Frequency:** Weekly during growing season
- **Existing solutions:** Google Sheets, paper maps, group chats
- **Why they fall short:** No purpose-built tool; everything is cobbled together
- **Visual style:** Seed Catalog Botanical — vintage seed packet illustrations, kraft paper textures, botanical green palette

### 5. RentalWalk
- **Pain:** Property managers doing move-in/move-out walkthroughs use paper checklists and phone photos that are impossible to organize. Deposit disputes are constant because documentation is poor.
- **Target:** Small-to-mid property managers managing 20-200 units
- **Frequency:** Every tenant turnover (weekly for larger operations)
- **Existing solutions:** Inspection apps like HappyCo, ZInspector — enterprise-priced
- **Why they fall short:** $100+/month minimums; overkill for small operators; require training
- **Visual style:** Blueprint Clipboard — construction blueprint grid background, red annotation marks, photo attachment zones

### 6. FormDrill
- **Pain:** Group fitness coaches (CrossFit, HIIT, martial arts) can't quickly log attendance AND performance for 10-30 people per class. Existing tools focus on one or the other.
- **Target:** Group fitness coaches / box owners (~50K CrossFit coaches + countless HIIT/martial arts)
- **Frequency:** Every class (2-5x daily)
- **Existing solutions:** WODify, Zen Planner (box management, expensive), paper whiteboards
- **Why they fall short:** Full gym management is overkill; coaches just want fast tap-to-log attendance + quick performance notes
- **Visual style:** Training Board — whiteboard marker on dark surface, neon chalk accents, athlete number badges

### 7. VetDose
- **Pain:** Veterinary technicians must calculate drug dosages on the fly for animals ranging from 2kg cats to 60kg dogs. Mental math errors are dangerous. Existing calculators are buried in clunky apps.
- **Target:** Veterinary technicians (~110K in US)
- **Frequency:** Every patient, multiple times daily
- **Existing solutions:** VetCalculators app, Plumb's app (subscription), mental math
- **Why they fall short:** Too many taps to get to the calculator; drug databases are behind paywalls; UX feels like a 2012 medical app
- **Visual style:** Pharmacy Label — prescription label aesthetic, amber bottle accents, clinical white surfaces

---

## Winner: MedHuddle

**Why this one:**
- Massive, underserved market (140K US residents, growing annually)
- Pain is acute and recurring (ACGME compliance is non-negotiable)
- Clear freemium revenue path (free logging → paid reports/export)
- Existing solutions are institution-first, not resident-first — classic "bottom-up SaaS" opportunity
- Would absolutely get upvoted on Product Hunt (medical trainees are vocal, tech-savvy, and networked)
- Strong visual identity opportunity (clinical clipboard/chart aesthetic — distinct from everything in the style log)

**Port:** 9700
**Container:** medhuddle
