Horizontal bars show current stock level relative to reorder point. Blue marker = reorder point. Dashed red = safety stock boundary. Items at or below the reorder point need action now.

SafeStock uses the standard safety stock formula: SS = Z × σd × √L, where Z is the service factor, σd is daily demand standard deviation, and L is lead time in days. EOQ uses the Wilson formula: √(2DS/H).
