## Brainstorm (7 Ideas)

1. **BuzzKill** — Corporate speak decoder. Paste jargon, get a BS Score, plain English translation, and a buzzword Hall of Shame. Pop Art / comic book style.

2. **Tab Therapy** — Enter your open browser tabs; an AI "therapist" gives a psychological profile of your procrastination patterns. "You have 14 tabs about learning Portuguese you've had open since 2023."

3. **The Regret Calculator** — Enter a life decision you're considering; get regret probability projections at 1/5/10/20 years based on psychological research on regret patterns (reversible vs irreversible, action vs inaction).

4. **Meeting Minutes Autopsy** — Paste any meeting transcript or notes; AI identifies what was actually *decided* vs endlessly *discussed* vs skillfully *avoided*. With a "time wasted" estimate.

5. **Excuse Evaluator** — Enter any excuse for anything (being late, not finishing something). Rated scientifically on plausibility (0-100), originality (how many people have used this), and believability. Issues a "Excuse Certificate."

6. **The Hype Decay Index** — Personal Gartner-style hype cycle tracker. Add your interests/obsessions and mark where they are on your personal hype cycle. Track how long things take from "Peak Enthusiasm" to "Plateau of Productivity."

7. **Plain English Ranker** — Paste any document; finds the most unnecessarily complex sentence, rates its "obfuscation score," and offers a plain English rewrite. Leaderboard of worst corporate sentences of the day.

## Why BuzzKill Won

- **Viral potential is #1**: People would share screenshots of their boss's emails on LinkedIn (ironically). It would trend on Twitter/X, Product Hunt, HN.
- **Real utility**: Everyone has to deal with corporate jargon. This solves a real (if comedic) pain point.
- **Unique aesthetic**: Pop Art / comic book is completely unexplored in the style log. Roy Lichtenstein meets LinkedIn satire.
- **No dependencies**: Works entirely with a built-in 200+ buzzword dictionary — no AI API needed, no latency.
- **Shareable output**: The BS Score + KAPOW! explosion is inherently screenshot-worthy.
- **B2B appeal**: Could be a real SaaS for HR/comms teams wanting to improve internal communication clarity.

## Technical Notes

- Node.js + Express backend, pure vanilla HTML/CSS/JS frontend
- 200+ buzzword dictionary with length-sorted matching to prevent false positives
- Translation is built-in (no external AI) — fast, reliable, works offline
- BS Score formula: density component (max 60) + unique buzzwords (max 25) + volume (max 15) = 0-100
- Network pool issue on airstream resolved with `network_mode: host`
- Deployed as `buzzkill` container on airstream port 3135
- Screenshot taken, added to servershed apps.json (sorted public-first, newest first)
