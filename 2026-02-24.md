## Brainstorm

Five ideas considered before selecting:

1. **Boondock Budget** — Nomad lifestyle financial tracker. Track income (consulting, content) and RV-specific expenses (fuel/campground/propane/repairs). Cost-per-day metric, runway calculator, 6-month chart.

2. **Solar Day Planner** — Off-grid power budget tool. Input battery %, solar capacity, today's sun hours, planned appliances → daily power plan. Great for Airstream but narrow audience.

3. **Consulting Rate Optimizer** — IT freelancer billing rate calculator with break-even analysis. Useful for Brian but very niche beyond Nutanix consultants.

4. **RV Route Planner** — Campground stop planner with RV-specific routing (height, propane restrictions). Requires mapping APIs, more complex to build correctly.

5. **Newsletter Content Calendar** — Substack idea queue and writing tracker. Useful but competes with too many existing tools.

---

## Why Boondock Budget?

**Strongest on both criteria:** directly benefits Brian AND has real SaaS potential.

- Brian is a full-time nomad managing freelance consulting income (Nutanix), a growing newsletter (Nomads Edge), and significant nomad expenses
- Existing financial tools (YNAB, Mint, Copilot) are not designed for nomad life — they don't have categories for "Campground/Site," "Propane," or income from "Content/Newsletter"
- The "Cost Per Day" metric and "Runway Days" calculator are the two numbers every full-timer lives by — no existing tool foregrounds these
- SaaS path: full-time RV community is ~1 million Americans and growing. Newsletter + community = distribution. Premium features (multi-trip analysis, tax prep, sync across devices) = revenue.
- Fully buildable as client-side app with localStorage — no backend complexity, no API dependencies

## Design Direction: Desert Expedition Journal

**Anti-sameness check:** Previous builds have used dark-glassmorphism, vintage Tumblr, Swiss brutalism, engineering blueprint, Art Deco cinema, cassette punk, Memphis design, Soviet constructivism, tabloid sensationalism, and enterprise dashboard. "Desert Expedition Journal" fills an unexplored slot.

- **Background:** Warm sand #F4E6C0 with subtle graph paper grid overlay
- **Cards:** Aged parchment #EDD9A3 with tan #B89050 borders
- **Expenses:** Rust terracotta #C0532A (evokes desert clay)
- **Income:** Sage green #5A7A50 (desert scrub vegetation)
- **Typography:** Arvo (slab serif, expedition manual feel) + IBM Plex Mono (instruments/data)
- **Signature element:** Daily Burn Rate Compass — a circular canvas-drawn gauge styled as an expedition navigation compass. Tick marks, rotating needle, arc fill, and color-coded status badge (Under Budget ✓ / Near Budget ⚠ / Over Budget !). Transforms a mundane metric into a thematic navigation instrument.
- **Secondary signatures:** Graph paper texture on the add-entry form; rubber stamp decorations on card headers ("FIELD LOG"); expedition logo mark
