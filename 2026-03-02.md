## Brainstorm

Five ideas considered across completely different domains:

1. **Dead Letters** — Private unsent letter app. Write to exes, the departed, past/future selves, regrets. 1940s airmail aesthetic with UNDELIVERABLE stamp animation. Zero servers, pure localStorage.

2. **The Flavor Graph** — Cocktail/food ingredient pairing visualizer. Interactive force-directed graph showing flavor compound affinities. Targets mixologists and adventurous cooks.

3. **Mundane Manifesto** — AI writes a passionate philosophical defense of your most ordinary personal habit (your coffee ritual, your commute route, how you load the dishwasher).

4. **The Constraint Engine** — Daily creative challenge generator. Writers get word constraints, artists get technique limits, musicians get time signatures. Serves creative professionals with fresh challenges every day.

5. **Argument Court** — Two parties describe a disagreement; app renders a mock legal verdict with printable judgment card. Would destroy at parties.

## Why Dead Letters Won

- **Universal human experience** — everyone has something they never said
- **Zero overlap** with any existing app factory builds (no RV, no camping, no finance)
- **Product Hunt bait** — "Private. Beautiful. Unsent." is a perfect three-word pitch
- **Emotional resonance** — apps that make people feel something get shared
- **Visual potential** — 1940s airmail aesthetic is untouched in the style log
- **Privacy-first** — zero backend needed makes it instantly trustworthy
- **Therapeutic use case** — grief counselors, therapists, writing coaches all recommend "write a letter you never send"

---

## Design Direction

**Style:** Wartime Airmail Romance — 1940s U.S. Dead Letter Office  
**Palette:** Cream parchment, airmail blue, vermillion red, stamp gold, aged ink  
**Typography:** Playfair Display (headings/italic), Special Elite (typewriter body), IM Fell English (letter text), Bebas Neue (rubber stamp)  
**Signature element:** RETURN TO SENDER circular rubber stamp that slams in with animation when you open a letter — scale from 1.6x with rotation easing down to final position  

---

## Tech Stack

- Pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript (zero framework, zero dependencies)
- Browser localStorage (zero telemetry, zero backend, privacy by design)
- nginx:alpine serving static files
- Docker on airstream port 3136
