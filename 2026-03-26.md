# App Factory — 2026-03-26

## Brainstorm

### 1. Email Subject Line Tester (newsletter/email marketers)
**Pain:** Subject lines get silently truncated at different lengths across Gmail mobile (~41 chars), Gmail desktop (~70 chars), Apple Mail mobile (~38 chars). Writers blindly hit send not knowing if half their subject got cut off on most devices. Existing tools (Litmus, Email on Acid) are enterprise-priced.
**Who has it:** Every Substack, Beehiiv, Mailchimp user — tens of millions of people who send email newsletters. It's a weekly problem.
**Existing solution quality:** Poor. Most tools are buried in paid platforms. No clean free standalone tool exists.

### 2. Physical Therapy Home Exercise Program Card Generator
**Pain:** PTs spend 15-20 minutes per patient manually building exercise handout PDFs from clipart + Word documents. The software they have (Clinicient, TherapyNotes) charges extra for it or does it poorly.
**Who has it:** ~200,000 PTs in the US, most in small clinics without enterprise software budgets.
**Why not picked:** Output requires high-quality exercise illustrations to be useful; building good art assets is out of scope for one night.

### 3. Real Estate Commission Split Calculator
**Pain:** Team-based agents manually do the math on every deal: team split + brokerage cap + franchise fee + E&O = confusing. One wrong input and the agent over/under-reports to their CPA.
**Who has it:** ~1.5M US real estate agents on teams.
**Why not picked:** The core logic is relatively simple; not enough substance for a night's worth of visual design and engineering.

### 4. SaaS License Audit Wall (SMB ops leads)
**Pain:** Most companies have 30-50 SaaS subscriptions. Nobody tracks when renewals hit, whether you're using the tier you pay for, or who even owns the login.
**Who has it:** Ops managers, IT leads at companies 5-150 people.
**Why not picked:** Needs persistent data storage with user accounts to be genuinely useful. Too much backend for Tier 1.

### 5. Punch List Tracker for Small Contractors
**Pain:** Final punch list walkthroughs before project handover are managed in PDFs, emails, or paper. Procore is $375/month minimum — overkill for a 3-person GC.
**Who has it:** Small general contractors, custom home builders, remodelers.
**Why not picked:** Photo upload requirement (photos are the whole point on a punch list) makes this more complex than a one-night build warrants.

---

## Selection: **#1 — Email Subject Line Tester ("Preflight")**

**Why this one wins:**

1. **GateMate-level specificity:** "Does my subject line get cut off on iPhone?" is Googled thousands of times a week. The pain is specific, recurring, and universally relatable to the target audience.

2. **No good free solution:** EmailOnAcid and Litmus are $100-200/month enterprise tools. Substack writers and indie newsletter operators have nothing standalone and free.

3. **Weekly recurring use:** Anyone sending a newsletter (daily, weekly, monthly) hits this exact problem every single time they write a subject line.

4. **Pure frontend:** No backend, no auth, no database — ships fast, works reliably, zero ongoing cost.

5. **Product Hunt-worthy:** The concept is immediately understandable and shareable. "A tool that shows you exactly how your subject line looks in Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook before you send" is a one-sentence Product Hunt pitch.

6. **Clear freemium path:** Free tier (3 inbox previews, basic analysis), Pro tier ($5-9/month: unlimited saved drafts, A/B test comparison mode, Outlook preview, team sharing).
