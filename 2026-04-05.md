# App Factory — 2026-04-05

## Brainstorm (problem-first)

1) **StickerTally (SLP)** — *touch-first session tallies + quick progress trends*
   - **Pain point:** SLPs need to tally trials in real time; paper sheets get lost and EHR screens are too slow to tap mid-session.
   - **Who has it:** Speech-language pathologists (schools, private clinics, outpatient).
   - **Frequency:** Every session, all week.

2) **PrepLabel** — *compliant prep-label generator for small kitchens*
   - **Pain point:** Food prep labels (prep date/use-by/allergens) are messy and inconsistent; health inspections ding teams.
   - **Who has it:** Small restaurants, coffee shops, commissary kitchens.
   - **Frequency:** Daily.

3) **ChairTurn** — *waitlist + turnover timer for small barbershops*
   - **Pain point:** Managing a walk-in list and communicating “you’re up next” is chaotic without a host stand.
   - **Who has it:** Barbershops and small salons.
   - **Frequency:** Daily.

4) **BinderScan QC** — *print shop preflight checklist that catches "oops" errors*
   - **Pain point:** Shops lose time/money to preventable mistakes (bleed, spine width, overprint, missing fonts).
   - **Who has it:** Small print shops / indie book printers.
   - **Frequency:** Weekly/daily.

5) **ShiftBrief** — *handoff notes template + timer for clinical on-call*
   - **Pain point:** Shift handoffs are inconsistent; critical info falls through the cracks.
   - **Who has it:** On-call nurses/PA/clinic teams.
   - **Frequency:** Every shift.

6) **FieldCal** — *drop-rate + calibration log for pest control techs*
   - **Pain point:** Calibration math and documentation are annoying and often done sloppily in the field.
   - **Who has it:** Pest control technicians and supervisors.
   - **Frequency:** Weekly/monthly.

## Picked idea (best problem/solution fit)
**StickerTally (SLP)**.

Why it won:
- Extremely clear, recurring pain (every session) with a known “bad current solution” (paper and sluggish EHR UI).
- A lightweight tool can genuinely save time and frustration without requiring integrations.
- Clean freemium path: free local-only; paid adds multi-device sync, printable SOAP note templates, clinic dashboards.

## Tier 1 build notes
- **Design direction:** "Clinical Clipboard + Highlighter" (ruled paper panels + die-cut goal stickers + pencil tally marks)
- **Architecture:** React + TypeScript SPA, localStorage persistence, no backend.
