# App Factory Build — March 25, 2026

## Brainstorm: 7 Problem-First Ideas

### 1. PrintRun — Indie Author Book Pricing Calculator
**Pain point:** Self-published authors set their cover price wrong because the math spans multiple vendor dashboards, royalty tables, and spreadsheets no one has pre-assembled. Most first-timers either overprice (kills sales) or underprice (earns nothing after print cost).
**Who has it:** The ~1.7M Americans who self-publish annually, particularly first-time authors using IngramSpark + KDP. Also writing coaches who advise clients.
**Existing solutions:** IngramSpark's royalty calculator (single-channel, no pre-pub cost math). KDP's calculator (same). No multi-channel break-even tool exists.

### 2. RotationBoard — Youth Sports Playing Time Tracker
**Pain point:** Youth sports coaches track player substitutions via pen and paper. Parents notice when their kid sits for three quarters. Fairness tracking is entirely manual.
**Who has it:** Youth soccer, basketball, and baseball coaches (millions of them). Especially volunteer coaches with no staff.
**Existing solutions:** Generic timer apps, nothing sport-specific with fairness alerts.

### 3. ClipNote — Video Editing Timestamp Tagger
**Pain point:** Editing a long interview? You need to tag moments ("great soundbite," "cut this," "B-roll needed") while the video plays — without being inside Premiere or DaVinci.
**Who has it:** Content creators, journalists, documentary editors, podcast producers.
**Existing solutions:** DaVinci marker system (requires being in editor), Notion (not synced to video), nothing lightweight and browser-based.

### 4. IngredientSwap — Recipe Substitution Calculator
**Pain point:** Recipe calls for buttermilk, you have none. Existing substitution charts are static — they don't scale quantities.
**Who has it:** Home cooks, specifically anyone mid-recipe who doesn't want to go to the store.
**Existing solutions:** Dozens of static lookup tables; none with quantity scaling or "what do I actually have?" logic.

### 5. PaceGraph — Segmented Race Pace Planner
**Pain point:** No tool lets runners plan a segmented race strategy (miles 1-6 at 9:30, miles 7-13 at 9:00, fade at mile 11 to 10:30) and calculate the resulting finish time vs. goal. All calculators assume uniform pace.
**Who has it:** Runners training for half marathons and marathons who know negative-split or fade strategies.
**Existing solutions:** Pace calculators all assume uniform pace. Zero segmented planners exist.

### 6. StagePack — Touring Musician Tech Rider Manager
**Pain point:** Gigging bands have gear promises per venue (what you bring, what they have, workarounds). Tracked in notes apps or not at all.
**Who has it:** Local and regional bands, touring musicians in the $50-500/night range.
**Existing solutions:** None purpose-built for small touring acts.

### 7. ServicePrice — Freelance Service Rate Calculator
**Pain point:** Plumbers, cleaners, tutors, and tradespeople price new service types by gut feel or competitor copying. Calculating correctly from labor + materials + overhead + profit margin is spreadsheet work they don't do.
**Who has it:** Independent service providers across all trades.
**Existing solutions:** Generic markup calculators; nothing service-type-specific with overhead factors.

---

## Winner: PrintRun

**Why this one:**
1. **Brian is the target user** — he has four published books and navigated exactly this confusion. He knows the pain intimately.
2. **No good existing solution** — IngramSpark and KDP each have single-channel royalty calculators but nothing handles multi-channel + pre-pub cost amortization + income goal planning in one place.
3. **Immediately useful, used repeatedly** — every book is a new break-even calculation. Authors return for each title.
4. **Clear Product Hunt hook** — "Finally, the real indie publishing math in one calculator" is an obvious upvote headline.
5. **Freemium path** — save/export projects, multi-book portfolio, per-channel revenue projections, ISBN lookup = natural paid tier.
6. **Completely outside the RV/travel space** — lateral thinking working exactly as intended.

## Build Notes

- **URL:** http://100.97.65.28:3240
- **Container:** `printrun`
- **Style:** Penguin Modern Classics Grid (1935 Edward Young three-band design)
- **Signature element:** Live Penguin-style book cover that updates as user types
- **Deployment:** Docker on airstream, custom `10.200.240.0/28` subnet (all 172.x and 192.168.x ranges exhausted on airstream)
- **Channels included:** IngramSpark, Amazon KDP, Direct/Events, Gumroad, Draft2Digital, Bookshop.org
- **Print cost model:** IngramSpark POD formula (base + per-page × page count, by interior type + size)
- **Network issue:** All default Docker bridge subnets exhausted on airstream — used `10.200.240.0/28` custom subnet
