# App Factory — 2026-03-23

## Brainstorm

**7 Problem-First Ideas Evaluated:**

1. **OverScope** (Freelancers) — Scope creep tracker. 59M US freelancers lose money every month because they don't log "just one more thing" requests. By project end they've done $3,000+ of free work. No focused free tool exists. *Pain: losing real income. Who: all independent contractors. Frequency: every single project.*

2. **LessonLog** (Private music teachers) — Lesson + student progress tracker for independent instructors. Teachers with 20+ students use notebooks or memory. Competitors charge $20-$25/month. *Pain: no good free tool. Who: 4M+ US private instructors. Frequency: weekly.*

3. **PrintQuote** (Small custom apparel/print shops) — Cost calculator for screen printing quotes. 50-shirt order with 3 print positions + setup fees + garment cost = constant underquoting. *Pain: shops lose money on underquotes. Who: hundreds of thousands of small shops. Frequency: daily.*

4. **DraftLog** (Craft bar owners) — Keg level tracker, pour cost analyzer, tap rotation manager. Bars lose 15-20% of keg revenue to waste. *Pain: real revenue leak. Who: thousands of craft bars. Frequency: daily.*

5. **VetEstimate** (Pet owners) — Side-by-side comparison of two vet treatment estimates when getting a second opinion, with medical term explainers. *Pain: impossible to compare apples-to-apples. Who: any pet owner facing expensive treatment. Frequency: low, but emotionally high-stakes.*

6. **ChordMap** (Musicians) — Transpose and visualize chord progressions for learning songs in a different key. *Pain: Transposer.com is ugly and does one thing. Who: gigging musicians, students. Frequency: weekly.*

7. **ScopeGuard** (Agencies) — SOW tracking + change order management for agencies. More enterprise-y version of OverScope. *Pain: overlap with #1, less focused.*

## Chosen: OverScope

**Why this one:**
- Strongest emotional resonance: every freelancer knows exactly what scope creep feels like
- The quantification hook is the killer feature — "you've given away $1,847 of free work on this project" hits hard
- Nothing good and free exists (there are $30-$50/month PM tools with scope tracking buried in them, but no focused standalone tool)
- Clear freemium path: free tier = 2 projects, Pro = $12/month for unlimited + PDF export + invoice integration
- Completely outside Brian's daily world but lands immediately for any consultant
- Highest "would get upvoted on Product Hunt" potential of the seven

## Build Summary

**App:** OverScope — Scope Creep Tracker & Change Order Generator for Freelancers
**Port:** 3238 (airstream)

**Container:** overscope

**Visual Style:** Litigation Brief
- Cream legal paper background, double-rule section dividers, case file numbering system
- Rubber-stamp EXHIBIT markers (A, B, C...) with slam-in animation on each scope event
- Section notation: § I (Exhibits), § II (File New Exhibit), § III (Change Order Generator)
- Typography: Playfair Display (headings) + IM Fell English (italic accents) + IBM Plex Mono (data)
- Color: court red for exposure figures, legal green for "clean" projects
- Anti-sameness: completely different from all 30+ previous builds — first legal-document aesthetic in log

**Features Built:**
1. Case Files (projects) with client, rate, contract value, original scope
2. Exhibit logging with category (Feature/Revision/Meeting/Support/Spec/Other), hours, date, "Client Requested" flag
3. Real-time exposure calculation (hours × rate)
4. Case vitals dashboard: total exposure, exhibit count, hourly rate, % overage vs contract
5. Header stats: open cases, total exhibits, total $ exposure across all projects
6. Professional change order email generator — one click generates ready-to-send email
7. Copy-to-clipboard for change order
8. Project status: Active / Disputed / Closed
9. Sample data seeded on first run (Henderson Insurance Website Redesign example)
10. Full localStorage persistence — no account, no server
