## Winner: StarChart

**Why this one:** Brian has a Dwarf 3 smart telescope. Every clear night, the question is: "What should I photograph?" The answer requires knowing your location, tonight's twilight times, moon phase, which objects are above the horizon and when they peak, and what camera settings to use. Existing tools (Stellarium, SkySafari) are powerful but overwhelming. StarChart is the focused, opinionated, beautiful answer.

**SaaS potential:** Free tier → personal use. Pro tier ($5-8/month) → cloud sync of your sessions, weather integration, multi-telescope profiles, personalized recommendations based on imaging history, community ratings of targets. The photography/astronomy community is passionate and pays for good tools.

## Build

- **App:** StarChart — Tonight's Astrophotography Planner
- **Port:** 3133 (airstream)
- **URL:** http://100.97.65.28:3133
- **Tech:** Pure HTML/CSS/JS (vanilla), nginx, Docker
- **Visual Style:** "Celestial Vellum" — 17th century celestial atlas on aged parchment
- **Build time:** ~2 hours
- **Notable technical achievements:**
  - Full spherical astronomy engine in vanilla JS (GMST, hour angle, alt/az transform)
  - Binary-search twilight crossing times (civil, nautical, astronomical)
  - Simplified lunar position + phase calculation
  - Rise/set time finder via altitude sampling + interpolation
  - Per-object altitude curves sampled every 20 minutes through dark window
  - All calculations run client-side, zero backend

## Visual Identity

- **Style:** Celestial Vellum — 17th century celestial atlas meets modern astronomy UX
- **Palette:** Aged vellum (#F6EDD4), Prussian blue (#1A3055), antique gold (#C9A84C), dark sepia (#2C1A0E), cream card (#FAF5E8)
- **Typography:** Playfair Display (serif headers, italic titles) + EB Garamond (body, descriptions) + Space Mono (coordinates, data, settings)
- **Signature element:** Interactive SVG sky dome styled as a historical celestial globe — altitude rings at 30°/60°/90°, cardinal direction labels, cross-hair guides, atlas corner ornaments, color-coded DSO dots with type-based colors. Aged paper CSS texture via SVG filter. Double inset border frame around entire viewport (atlas page feel).
- **Color completely distinct from all prior builds** — warm ivory/parchment base is opposite to every dark-themed prior app
