## Brainstorm: 5 Problem-First Ideas

### 1. On-Call Rotation Builder (DevOps / Engineering Leads)
**Pain:** Small engineering teams (3–15 people) manage on-call schedules in Google Sheets. Someone always gets too many weekends. Holidays are missed. PagerDuty is $21/user/month — overkill for a 6-person startup.  
**Who:** Engineering leads and DevOps managers at companies with 3–25 person teams.  
**Existing solution gap:** PagerDuty/OpsGenie are enterprise-priced. Free alternatives require account setup, API integrations, or are buried in larger monitoring platforms. No clean, free, no-account builder exists.

### 2. SLA Calculator for SaaS Founders
**Pain:** "99.9% uptime" sounds fine until you realize it's 8.7 hrs/year of acceptable downtime. Founders negotiate SLAs without understanding the operational and financial implications.  
**Who:** Early-stage SaaS founders, engineering managers negotiating enterprise contracts.  
**Existing solution:** A few blog posts and calculators exist. Problem is somewhat solved.

### 3. Permit Progress Tracker for General Contractors
**Pain:** GCs juggle 10+ active permits per job — each with different inspection windows, status, expiry dates. They do this in email chains and sticky notes. Permits expire. Inspections get missed. Jobs stall.  
**Who:** Small GCs with residential or light commercial work.  
**Gap:** Good. Most permit tracking is buried inside enterprise construction PM tools (Procore, $600+/mo).

### 4. Scope of Work Builder for Service Businesses
**Pain:** HVAC, plumbing, and IT service companies write SOWs by hand for very similar jobs. Proposals take 2 hours instead of 15 minutes due to zero templating.  
**Who:** Owner-operated service businesses.  
**Gap:** Good but competitive — Jobber, Housecall Pro, etc. address this with full-platform approaches.

### 5. Interview Scorecard Builder
**Pain:** Hiring managers run unstructured interviews with no rubric per role. Candidates get inconsistent experiences. Bias creeps in. You hire on gut feeling.  
**Who:** Startup hiring managers, HR generalists at 20–200 person companies.  
**Gap:** Exists but often bundled into ATS tools. Standalone free tool could get traction.

---

## Selection: #1 — On-Call Rotation Builder (RotaCycle)

**Why this one:**
- The gap between "Google Sheet" and "PagerDuty at $21/user/month" is massive and real
- Every engineering team with on-call duties has this problem, and most teams under 20 people won't pay enterprise tool prices
- Clearly identifiable target user: engineering leads at small companies
- Weekly recurring use (schedule is regenerated each quarter, shifts are checked weekly)
- Clear freemium path: free web tool → SaaS with Slack/PagerDuty integration → team calendar sync
- No good existing free standalone solution
- Passes the GateMate standard: specific audience, real recurring frustration, no adequate existing free tool

## Design Direction

**Style:** "Olivetti Modernist Office Machine"  
**Inspiration:** Olivetti typewriters, calculators, and early computing machinery of the 1950s–70s Italian industrial design era  
**Palette:** Cream (#F5F0E8), Olivetti black (#1A1410), Valentine red (#C8232C), Chrome (#B8B0A8), Tape white (#FFFDF8)  
**Typography:** Barlow Condensed (headers/labels), Space Grotesk (body/UI), Space Mono (numbers/dates)  
**Signature element:** Paper tape output strip — calendar views rendered inside a "tape" div with sprocket-hole rows at top and bottom, color-coded shift blocks rendered as typewriter ink on paper tape

## Implementation Summary

- Pure HTML/CSS/JavaScript (zero runtime dependencies)
- Three rotation patterns: Weekly, Daily, Custom N-day
- Three scheduling algorithms: Fair Distribution (equalize), Round Robin, Weighted
- Calendar view with month navigation
- Week Strip view — matrix showing ON/BCK per person per day
- Fairness view — bar charts for primary, weekend, holiday, and backup shift distribution
- Swap tool — reassign any two dates for vacation coverage
- One-click US 2026 federal holiday load
- ICS export (grouped by person, compatible with Google/Apple/Outlook)
- CSV export
- Print support
- localStorage persistence (no server, no account)
- Docker deploy via nginx:alpine
