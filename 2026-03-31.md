# App Factory Build — 2026-03-31

## Brainstorm (Problem-First)

1. **BoxRate — Dimensional Weight Calculator** (SELECTED)
   - **Pain point:** Carriers (UPS, FedEx, USPS) charge by dimensional weight when it exceeds actual weight, and each uses a different divisor (139 vs 166). Shippers constantly get surprised by inflated bills because they didn't check DIM weight before choosing a carrier.
   - **Who has it:** Anyone who ships packages regularly — eBay/Etsy sellers, small business owners, Poshmark resellers, warehouse staff, anyone doing holiday shipping. Millions of people.
   - **Frequency:** Every time they ship something. Weekly or more for small business sellers.
   - **Existing solutions:** Carrier websites calculate their own DIM weight but don't compare across carriers side-by-side. No simple tool shows "which carrier is cheapest for THIS box?"

2. **InvoiceLint — Invoice Error Checker**
   - Pain point: Freelancers and small businesses send invoices with math errors, wrong rates, missing tax calculations.
   - Who: Freelancers, contractors, small businesses.
   - Rejected: Too close to finance/budget tracker territory.

3. **StagePlot — Live Music Stage Layout Builder**
   - Pain point: Musicians drawing stage plots on napkins before gigs.
   - Who: Working musicians, venue managers.
   - Rejected: Overlaps with PatchSheet territory (live sound tools).

4. **CertTrack — Professional Certification Tracker**
   - Pain point: Professionals losing track of when certifications expire, missing renewal deadlines.
   - Who: IT pros, healthcare workers, electricians, pilots.
   - Rejected: Decent idea but less universal than BoxRate.

5. **DyeCalc — Fabric Dye Ratio Calculator**
   - Pain point: Fiber artists and garment dyers constantly calculating dye-to-water-to-fiber ratios.
   - Who: Dyers, textile artists.
   - Rejected: Too niche, small user base.

6. **FuelSplit — Road Trip Fuel Cost Splitter**
   - Pain point: Splitting gas costs fairly on road trips based on who drove which legs, vehicle MPG, fuel prices.
   - Who: Road trippers, college students, travel groups.
   - Rejected: Too close to budget/finance territory.

7. **PortfolioWeight — Investment Rebalancing Calculator**
   - Pain point: DIY investors calculating how much to buy/sell to rebalance to target allocations.
   - Who: Self-directed investors.
   - Rejected: Finance territory, explicitly banned.

## Why BoxRate Won

BoxRate solves a specific, recurring frustration that affects a massive audience (anyone who ships packages). The problem is easy to explain: "I got charged $45 instead of $20 because the carrier used dimensional weight, and I didn't know." No existing tool compares DIM weight across all three major US carriers side-by-side. The tool is useful every time someone ships — weekly or more for small business sellers. Clear freemium path: free basic calculator → premium API for ecommerce integrations, batch calculations, rate optimization.
