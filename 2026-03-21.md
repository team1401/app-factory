## Why InkBook Won

**Problem/solution fit is the strongest:**
- The pain is real, recurring, and universal in the industry (every solo tattoo artist I could describe in one sentence)
- "They use Instagram DMs" is a known industry joke/complaint
- Deposit disputes and no-shows cost artists real money weekly
- Existing alternatives (Booksy, Square) aren't designed for the nuances of tattoo work: design briefs, multi-session pieces, skin type notes, allergy tracking
- Clear freemium revenue path: free local version → $12-20/month for cloud sync, SMS reminders, deposit payment links
- Could be described to any stranger: "you know how tattoo artists still take bookings by DM?" — instant recognition

## Design Direction

**Style:** Traditional Tattoo Flash Sheet  
Named after the laminated design sheets hung on tattoo shop walls showing available designs.

- **Palette:** Ink black (#1A1008), aged cream (#F5F0E0), traditional red (#C41A1A), green (#1A6B2A), gold (#D4A017), navy (#1A3A6B) — exactly the 6 colors of classic American traditional tattoo work
- **Typography:** Anton (condensed bold headlines — client names like stamps), Special Elite (handwritten notes/briefs feel), Oswald (UI labels), Pinyon Script (watermark)
- **Layout:** Appointment cards as flash panels, today's queue as a parlor call board with time-block column in deep black
- **Signature element:** Design briefs rendered in dashed-border italic Special Elite, as if handwritten on a job ticket. Corner ✦ ornaments on all cards. Horizontal ruled lines on the cream background = flash sheet paper.
- **Anti-sameness:** Zero dark backgrounds, zero glassmorphism, zero gradients. Pure flat ink-on-cream — first build in this aesthetic in the entire log.

## Features Built

- **Today's Queue** — sessions sorted by appointment time, deposit status indicators
- **Appointments** — full grid with search/filter, status pipeline, appointment detail modal
- **Clients** — table with history, total spent, skin type, allergy/medical notes
- **New Appointment Form** — piece description, style (13 options), placement (18 body positions), size, session type, pricing, deposit tracking, design brief notes
- **New Client Form** — phone, email, Instagram handle, skin type, allergy/medical notes, referral source
- **Status pipeline** — Pending → Deposit Paid → Confirmed → Complete / No Show / Cancelled
- **Financial tracking** — estimated price, deposit amount, deposit status, balance due auto-calculated
- **Studio Tools panel** — deposit calculator, time estimator, price calculator, printable aftercare guide
- **localStorage persistence** — no backend required, instant and private
- **Seeded demo data** — 3 clients, 4 appointments showing the app immediately
