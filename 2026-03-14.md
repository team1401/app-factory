## Brainstorm

| Idea | Pain Point | Target User |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **CloseRate** — Freelance Proposal Tracker | Freelancers send proposals and have no follow-up system — they give up after 1-2 touches, losing thousands | ~15M US freelancers, consultants, designers, agencies |
| **SealBook** — Notary Record Book | Notaries are legally required to maintain journals in many states but use paper notebooks that get lost | ~4.4M commissioned notaries in the US |
| **BlueBook** — Sports Official Game Log | Refs/umpires need to track games, fees, mileage for taxes and association reporting; done in paper notebooks | ~500K registered sports officials in the US |
| **MedShelf** — Caregiver Medication Cabinet | Adult children managing elderly parents' meds don't know what's expiring or running low | ~53M informal caregivers |
| **LandlordLog** — Tenant Communication Log | Small landlords handle things verbally then have no paper trail when disputes arise | ~18M small landlords (1-4 units) |
| **PriceCreep** — Subscription Price Audit | Streaming/SaaS services silently raise prices and you don't notice | Everyone with subscriptions |
| **MockLoop** — Interview Prep Tracker | Job seekers doing multiple interviews can't track what stories they've already told across companies | Active job seekers, especially tech workers in layoff cycles |

## Why CloseRate Won

- Every lost proposal = hundreds or thousands of dollars. Tying to money means people will actually pay for the tool.
- 15M+ freelancers is the broadest addressable market of the candidates
- Genuinely underserved: lightweight CRM niche is full of enterprise bloat; nothing purpose-built for proposal tracking + follow-up discipline
- Clear $6-9/mo SaaS path — 200 users = $1,200+/mo
- Interesting design opportunity: "Mid-Century Sales Motivational Manual" hasn't been done
- Signature element: Closer's Batting Average — completely distinctive, hadn't been built before

## Design Direction

**Style:** Mid-Century Sales Motivational Manual  
**Inspiration:** 1960s IBM sales training binders, Dale Carnegie workbooks, Zig Ziglar coaching materials  
**Philosophy:** The app coaches you as it tracks you. Numbers are displayed like vintage baseball scorecards. Language is earnestly optimistic.

**Palette:**
- Background: #F3EBD8 (warm cream paper stock)
- Surface: #FFFBF4 (white paper)
- Primary: #C84B1A (burnt sienna — classic motivational poster orange-red)
- Navy: #1C2E4A (authority)
- Gold: #C9941A (wins, highlights)
- Green: #2D7D46 (won proposals)
- Red: #BE2020 (lost, overdue)

**Typography:** Anton (display) + Plus Jakarta Sans (body) + DM Mono (numbers)

**Signature Element:** The "Closer's Batting Average" widget — win rate displayed as a baseball batting average (.500) with progressive rank labels from ROOKIE through HALL OF FAMER. Paired with a Win Wall of deal tombstones (investment banking tombstone concept) for won proposals.

**Layout:** Two-panel — left panel (520px) with stats, batting widget, filter tabs, proposal list; right panel (flex) with detail view, touchpoint timeline, action buttons.

## Features Shipped

- Proposal pipeline: Draft → Sent → Followed Up → Negotiating → Won / Lost / Ghosted
- Follow-up reminders with overdue alerts (⚡ with pulse animation)
- Touchpoint timeline per proposal (sent, follow-up, meeting, note)
- Dashboard stats: pipeline value, win rate, overdue count, avg close time
- Closer's Batting Average widget with rank progression
- Win Wall — deal tombstone badges for won proposals
- Quick action buttons on cards (Follow Up, Won, ✗)
- Add/edit proposal modal with currency support (USD/EUR/GBP/CAD/AUD)
- Seed data (8 realistic proposals across all status stages)
- Local JSON file storage (Docker volume)
- Keyboard shortcuts: Escape to close/deselect, ⌘N new proposal

## Tech Stack

- Node.js + Express backend (server.js)
- Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS frontend (single file)
- JSON file storage with Docker volume persistence

## Revenue Potential

Strong. Clear freemium path:
- Free: up to 5 active proposals
- Pro ($6-8/mo): unlimited proposals, CSV/PDF export, email reminders, multiple pipelines
- Team ($15-20/mo): shared pipeline, team stats

A freelancer who wins one extra $3K project per year because CloseRate reminded them to follow up has already paid for years of the subscription. Very favorable ROI argument.
