# App Factory — 2026-03-27 — GigBook

## Brainstorm (Problem-First)

### 1. Working musicians — gig tracking & unpaid invoices
**Pain:** "I gigged 40+ times last year and have no idea what I earned, who still owes me, or what I played where."
**Who:** Gigging musicians — bar performers, session players, wedding/event musicians, cover bands
**Why no existing tool:** Setlist.fm = setlists only (no money), QuickBooks = overkill, Google Sheets = fragile, Notes = chaos

### 2. Mobile veterinarians — SOAP notes on farm calls
**Pain:** Farm vets write SOAP notes with clipboards in the field then transcribe them back at the office — doubling work.
**Who:** Large animal / equine vets doing ranch visits
**Why no existing tool:** Practice management software is $200+/month just for the notes module

### 3. Tile & flooring contractors — material quantity calculator
**Pain:** "I have to calculate waste factor, grout joint width, and cut patterns in my head on every job, then wonder why I run short."
**Who:** Independent tile contractors and flooring installers
**Why no existing tool:** The apps are either toy simple or contractor-software-complex with no middle ground

### 4. Voice actors — audition tracker with booking rate
**Pain:** "I submit 50 auditions a month and have no idea which casting directors respond, what my booking rate is, or which client types I convert."
**Who:** Working voice actors doing commercial, animation, audiobook, e-learning work
**Why no existing tool:** Spreadsheet hell with no feedback loop or rate analysis

### 5. Personal trainers — client program progress tracker
**Pain:** "I have 15 clients in 15 different notebooks and I have to flip through pages to find where Sarah is in her 8-week program."
**Who:** Independent personal trainers (non-gym software subscribers)
**Why no existing tool:** Gym management SaaS is $80+/month; paper notebooks don't scale

### 6. Venue sound engineers — stage plot + input list builder
**Pain:** "Bands still email me Word docs and hand-drawn napkins as 'stage plots.' I have to reconstruct a proper tech rider before every show."
**Who:** Live sound engineers at small/mid venues
**Why no existing tool:** Nothing simple for quick structured input lists that both band and engineer can read

### 7. Market farmers (CSA) — crop tracking + harvest timeline
**Pain:** "When a customer asks 'will you have tomatoes Saturday?' I'm literally guessing, because my planting dates are in a spiral notebook."
**Who:** Small CSA farmers and direct-market growers
**Why no existing tool:** Farm management software is enterprise-priced; nothing lightweight with a harvest date planner

---

## Selected Idea: GigBook (Option 1)

**Rationale:** Strongest "why doesn't this exist?" energy. Every gigging musician has this pain. The existing options are all wrong-size: Setlist.fm is too narrow (no money), QuickBooks is too broad (overkill), spreadsheets are fragile. GigBook hits the exact gap — business tracking designed for musicians, not accountants. The "who still owes me" use case alone is worth the tool. High return-visit potential: musicians come back every time they play a gig.

**One-sentence pitch:** "Never lose track of an unpaid gig again — log every booking, setlist, and payment in one place."

## Design Direction

**Style:** "1970s Concert Handbill" — Bill Graham Presents / Fillmore West poster aesthetic. Warm near-black (#0A0804) background, concert gold (#E8B840) primary, Fillmore orange (#E85820) secondary, electric blue (#3060D0). Anton + Bebas Neue + IM Fell English type trio.

**Signature element:** Each gig card is a mini concert poster — venue name in huge Anton all-caps, color accent bar at top coded by pay status (green=paid, orange=owed, blue=free, red=canceled), status badge styled as concert copy.

**Anti-sameness check:** No dark glassmorphism. No gradients. Warm black-brown palette, not cool gray. Completely different from all 30+ prior builds.

---

## Build Notes

- Single-page app, all data in localStorage — no backend needed
- Three views: Gig Cards, Ledger Table, Summary Dashboard
- Monthly earnings chart (12-month bar chart)
- Top venues ranked by booking count + revenue
- One-click "Mark Paid" on owed gig cards
- Setlist tracking per gig with pill tags
- Demo data seeded on first load (7 realistic gigs)
- Deployed via Docker/nginx to airstream port 3241
- Build time: ~45 minutes
