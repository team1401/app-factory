# App Factory Build — 2026-04-04

## Brainstorm (Problem-First)

1. **SplitSheet** — Music royalty split calculator for bands/collaborators using industry formulas (Nashville method). *Pain:* Bands argue over splits. *Who:* Independent musicians, songwriters. *Frequency:* Every session. *Gap:* Paid SaaS or generic spreadsheets. ❌ Too niche, low recurring use for most users.

2. **DyeCalc** — Hair dye mixing ratio calculator for stylists. Developer volume, target level, processing times. *Pain:* Wrong ratios waste product and damage hair. *Who:* Independent stylists, cosmetology students. *Frequency:* Daily. *Gap:* Brand-specific charts only. ❌ Very niche audience, brand-dependent formulas.

3. **ThreadCount** — Social media thread/carousel planner. Paste long content, split at sentence boundaries per platform limits. *Pain:* Manual splitting is tedious. *Who:* Content creators, marketers. *Frequency:* Weekly+. *Gap:* Bloated SaaS with required accounts. ❌ Platforms keep changing limits; many existing tools.

4. **SeedCal** — Seed starting date calculator. Enter USDA zone or frost date, select crops, get complete planting timeline. *Pain:* Gardeners cross-reference multiple sources for planting dates. *Who:* 40M+ home gardeners. *Frequency:* Seasonal but intense. *Gap:* Ugly extension PDFs or paid apps. ✅ **SELECTED**

5. **FadeCalc** — Espresso recipe dialing calculator. Log shot parameters, get grind adjustment recommendations based on extraction theory. *Pain:* Wastes expensive beans without system. *Who:* Home espresso enthusiasts. *Frequency:* Daily. *Gap:* Basic ratio calculators only. ❌ Small audience, subjective taste makes formulas imprecise.

6. **InkCost** — Tattoo pricing estimator. Dimensions, detail, placement, color, session count → fair price. *Pain:* Artists underprice; clients have no transparency. *Who:* Tattoo artists, apprentices. *Frequency:* Weekly. *Gap:* No calculators exist. ❌ Pricing too variable by region/reputation to formula-ize well.

7. **LumenPlan** — Room lighting layout calculator per IES standards. Room dimensions → lumen output, fixture count, spacing grid, bulb suggestions. *Pain:* Wrong bulbs = dim or harsh rooms. *Who:* Homeowners, designers, electricians. *Frequency:* Per project. *Gap:* Ugly engineering tools. ❌ Low frequency, one-time use per room.

## Why SeedCal Won

- **Seasonal urgency**: It's April — prime planting season. Immediate relevance.
- **Massive audience**: 40M+ US gardeners, plus global interest in food growing post-pandemic.
- **Recurring need**: Every spring and fall, gardeners re-plan. Bookmark-worthy.
- **No beautiful solution**: Extension office PDFs, clunky apps with required accounts, or expensive paid tools. Nothing fast, free, and beautiful.
- **Product Hunt potential**: Visual timeline is inherently shareable. Seasonal virality.
- **Real math**: Based on actual USDA zone frost dates and standard weeks-before/after-frost planting formulas from agricultural extension data.

## Design Direction

**Style: "Seed Packet Illustration"** — Inspired by vintage botanical seed packet art from the 1920s-40s. Warm cream (#FDF6E3) background like aged paper, earthy decorative borders, and category-colored crop cards reminiscent of mini seed packets. Headers in Playfair Display serif for that vintage feel. Data in clean Inter sans-serif. Color-coded categories: cool-season blue, warm-season orange, herbs green. Signature elements: decorative fleurons (❧) in the header, dotted-line detail rows, and a horizontal Gantt-style planting timeline with frost date markers.

## Architecture

- Single HTML file with embedded CSS/JS
- 30 crops across 3 categories (cool season, warm season, herbs) with real agricultural data
- USDA zones 3a–10b with average frost date lookup table
- Date math: weeks-before/after-frost calculations for indoor start, transplant, direct sow, and harvest windows
- Gantt-style timeline visualization with percentage-based positioning
- localStorage persistence of zone, frost dates, and crop selection
- CSV export with full planting schedule
- Print-optimized stylesheet
- Responsive down to mobile
- Sample data on first load: Zone 6a with 6 pre-selected popular crops
