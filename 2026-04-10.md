# App Factory Brainstorm — 2026-04-10

## The Mission
Identify a problem-first, niche-profession app idea with a clear freemium path, avoiding all previously built styles (Swiss, Memphis, Blueprint, etc.) and restricted industries (travel, finance, etc.).

## Brainstorming List

1. **App Name**: **BatchCheck**
   - **Pain Point**: Tracking temperature and humidity exposure for sensitive pharmaceutical or biological samples during transit or storage.
   - **Target User**: Lab Technicians & Biotech Logistics Managers.
   - **Frequency**: Continuous monitoring/logging.
   - **Existing Solutions**: Expensive, proprietary hardware-linked dashboards that are difficult to integrate with third-party logistics.
   - **Visual Style Concept**: "Clean Room Digital" — Sterile, high-visibility, blue/white palette, with glowing status indicators and "Hazard/Safe" alert overlays.

2. **App Name**: **YieldMap**
   - **Pain Point**: Correlating specific soil/nutrient inputs with harvest quality/yield in specialized high-value crops (e.g., specialty mushrooms, saffron).
   - **Target User**: Specialty Mycologists & Urban Farmers.
   - **Frequency**: Weekly during growing/harvest cycles.
   - **Existing Solutions**: Generic farm management software that is too "broadacre" and lacks the granular, spore-level or saffron-strand-level detail required.
   - **Visual Style Concept**: "Microscopic Detail" — Dark, organic, high-detail imagery, with data overlays looking like biological microscope slides (circular viewports, scale bars, staining colors).

3. **App Name**: **SetList Flow**
   - **Pain Point**: Managing complex, real-time cue changes (lighting, sound, pyrotechnics) for live theater or immersive performance art.
   - **Target User**: Stage Managers & Technical Directors.
   - **Frequency**: Every performance/rehearsal.
   - **Existing Solutions**: Paper scripts or heavy, expensive professional software (Qlab/etc.) that are difficult to share and update in real-time across a crew.
   - **Visual Style Concept**: "Stage Manager's Clipboard" — Rugged, high-visibility, "Sharpie-on-tape" aesthetic, using high-contrast colors (Neon Yellow/Black) for readability in dark environments.

4. **App Name**: **SpecimenScan**
   - **Pain Point**: Cataloging and identifying geological or mineral samples for field researchers.
   - **Target User**: Field Geologists & Mineralogists.
   - **Frequency**: Daily during field expeditions.
   - **Existing Solutions**: Manual field notebooks or generic photo apps that don't allow for structured metadata (hardness, luster, cleavage) tied to GPS and high-res macro imagery.
   - **Visual Style Concept**: "Field Journal" — Weathered, rugged, topographical map backgrounds, with "Stamp" and "Sketch" elements to mimic a researcher's field notebook.

5. **App Name**: **Permitting Pro**
   - **Pain Point**: Managing the complex, multi-agency permit workflow for temporary urban events (street festivals, pop-up markets).
   - **Target User**: Event Producers & Urban Planners.
   - **Frequency**: Weekly/Monthly during event planning seasons.
   - **Existing Solutions**: Fragmented government portals and email threads that make tracking approval status across Fire, Police, and Health departments a nightmare.
   - **Visual Style Concept**: "City Planning Schematic" — Architectural/Blueprint-lite, with isometric map views of event zones and "Permit Granted" rubber stamp animations.

6. **App Name**: **AuditTrail** (Note: Already exists in style log, but concept was repurposed for Art. Re-evaluating for a brand new concept)
   - **Pain Point**: Verifying chain of custody for physical assets.
   - **Target User**: Art Conservators.
   - **Visual Style**: Museum Registrar.
   - **Status**: *REJECTED* (Duplicate Concept).

## The Winner: **SetList Flow**

**Reasoning:**
SetList Flow addresses a high-frequency, high-stress workflow in a niche industry (live theater/performance). Stage managers need real-time, synchronized information across a technical crew (lighting, sound, pyro). Current solutions are either too analog (paper) or too siloed (expensive proprietary software). The "Stage Manager's Clipboard" visual style — rugged, high-visibility, and "Sharpie-on-tape" — provides a distinct, usable identity that hasn't been used. It has a clear freemium path: free for small community theater productions, with premium subscription tiers for professional touring companies requiring advanced synchronization and multi-user coordination.
