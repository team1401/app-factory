## Why "Skill Issue?" Won

**Shareability is everything.** People will screenshot the verdict and share it — "This app told me my divorce was a skill issue." The five distinct judge personalities (ex-military, scientist, Italian grandmother, startup founder, Stoic) each bring a completely different take on the same situation. This creates comedic tension and genuine insight simultaneously.

**Outside Brian's world:** Completely different from RV life, travel logging, dashboards, budgeting. This belongs on Reddit, Twitter, Product Hunt. A VP who can't figure out why her team underperforms could submit it. A developer blaming their tools could submit it. A person who can't stick to their gym routine could submit it.

**Monetizable:** Paid tier for detailed historical tracking, team "excuse audit" dashboards, API access, white-label for HR retrospective tooling.

**Viral mechanic:** Every verdict is a conversation starter. "What did the Stoic say?" is a question that writes itself.

## Design Direction

**Style: Bauhaus Functional**

Inspired by the Bauhaus school of design (Walter Gropius, Herbert Bayer, Wassily Kandinsky, László Moholy-Nagy):

- **Color:** The three Bauhaus primaries — Red (#BE1621), Blue (#1B4FA0), Yellow (#F5C400) — plus black and white. No other colors.
- **Typography:** Archivo Black (display, geometric sans-serif) + Space Grotesk (UI, modern geometric) + JetBrains Mono (data labels)
- **Geometry:** Every decorative element is a pure geometric shape — circle, triangle, square, line, dot. No gradients, no shadows, no glassmorphism.
- **Signature:** The hero Bauhaus composition featuring a large blue circle outline, red triangle, yellow square, black horizontal rule and dot — directly referencing Kandinsky's shape-color theory from "Point and Line to Plane." Each judge has a unique geometric SVG icon. Thick 4px black borders as structural element throughout.
- **First:** Only fully white-background, Bauhaus-primary-color app in the style log — anti-sameness confirmed.
