# App Factory Brainstorm — 2026-04-14

## Ideas Generated

### 1. GrindLog ⭐ SELECTED
- **Pain point:** Home baristas dial in a perfect espresso shot but forget the exact grind size, dose, yield, temp, and timing next time they open that bag. They waste $20–40 bags of specialty coffee re-dialing from scratch.
- **Target user:** Specialty coffee enthusiasts / home baristas who rotate through 2–5 single-origin beans monthly and own adjustable burr grinders.
- **Frequency:** Weekly — every time they switch beans or a bag sits unused for a few days.
- **Existing solutions & gaps:** Notes app (unstructured, no quick input), Filtru (overly complex, subscription-heavy), generic journal apps (not purpose-built for coffee variables). Nothing with fast tap-to-log, bean photo, and instant recipe recall.
- **Visual style:** "Craft Coffee Bag Label" — kraft paper brown (#C4A87A), rubber-stamped typography, stamp-circle roast badges, tan stitching borders, hand-lettered feel. Warm, tactile, artisanal.
- **Freemium path:** Free = 5 beans + unlimited recipes. Paid = unlimited beans, brew analytics, flavor wheel tracking, export.

### 2. FabricStash
- **Pain point:** Quilters and sewists buy duplicate fabric at the store because they can't remember what's already in their stash. Their fabric collection lives in opaque bins.
- **Target user:** Quilters, garment sewists, and fiber hobbyists with 50+ fabric pieces in their stash.
- **Frequency:** Every shopping trip (weekly/biweekly).
- **Existing solutions & gaps:** Spreadsheets (no photos, slow), Ravelry (knitting/yarn only, not fabric). No purpose-built fabric stash app with photo + yardage + location tracking.
- **Visual style:** "Textile Sample Book" — fabric swatch catalog with woven texture borders, measuring tape accent elements.
- **Freemium path:** Free = 25 fabrics. Paid = unlimited + shopping list + project planning.

### 3. DoseLog
- **Pain point:** Patients on complex medication schedules (titrations, varying daily doses, on/off weeks) can't track their regimens with basic pill reminder apps that only support "take X every Y hours."
- **Target user:** Chronic illness patients, clinical trial participants, post-surgical recovery patients.
- **Frequency:** Daily, multiple times per day.
- **Existing solutions & gaps:** Apple Reminders (too simple), MyTherapy (can't handle complex schedules like "2 on MWF, 3 on TThSat, skip week 3"), MediSafe (same limitation). None support true titration protocols.
- **Visual style:** "Pharmacy Prescription Label" — prescription bottle orange, medical white, typewriter monospace, warning label accents.
- **Freemium path:** Free = 3 medications. Paid = unlimited + doctor share + interaction warnings.

### 4. HiveMind
- **Pain point:** Beekeepers managing 5–50 hives lose track of which hive received which treatment and when. Missed treatments mean lost colonies.
- **Target user:** Hobbyist and sideliner beekeepers with 5–50 hives.
- **Frequency:** Weekly during active season (spring–fall).
- **Existing solutions & gaps:** Paper hive cards (lost, damaged), Beetight (dated UI, expensive subscription). No modern, mobile-first hive management tool.
- **Visual style:** "Naturalist Field Journal" — botanical illustration style with hand-drawn honeycomb elements, aged paper.
- **Freemium path:** Free = 5 hives. Paid = unlimited + analytics + weather integration.

### 5. FlatFile
- **Pain point:** Opening a 500MB+ CSV or JSON file crashes Excel. Non-developers have no easy way to explore large datasets without learning pandas/SQL.
- **Target user:** Analysts, marketers, researchers, and operations people who receive large data exports but aren't developers.
- **Frequency:** Weekly — whenever a new data export arrives.
- **Existing solutions & gaps:** Excel (crashes on large files), pandas (requires coding), DuckDB CLI (too technical). No simple drag-and-drop data viewer with instant schema detection.
- **Visual style:** "Dot-Matrix Printout" — continuous feed paper aesthetic, green monospace data, tear-off perforations.
- **Freemium path:** Free = 100MB files. Paid = unlimited size + charts + export.

### 6. RepBook
- **Pain point:** Powerlifters and weightlifters following structured programs (5/3/1, Texas Method, Sheiko) track workouts in messy spreadsheets. Existing fitness apps are bloated social platforms.
- **Target user:** Intermediate-to-advanced lifters following percentage-based programs.
- **Frequency:** 3–6 times per week (every training session).
- **Existing solutions & gaps:** Strong (too generic, subscription-gated), spreadsheets (manual, no mobile input), Fitbod (AI-driven, not program-based). No clean, program-first tracker.
- **Visual style:** "Gym Chalkboard" — white chalk on dark slate, handwritten numerals, plate-loading barbell icons.
- **Freemium path:** Free = 1 program template. Paid = unlimited templates + progression analytics + 1RM tracking.

---

## Decision: GrindLog

**Why it wins:**
1. **Sharpest problem/solution fit** — the pain is visceral (wasting $30 bags of coffee) and the solution is obvious (tap to log, tap to recall)
2. **Passionate, spending-heavy community** — specialty coffee enthusiasts already buy $200+ grinders and $15–25/bag beans
3. **Weekly+ usage frequency** — every bean rotation or dial-in session
4. **Clear freemium path** — free tier is genuinely useful, paid unlocks power-user features
5. **Product Hunt appeal** — niche but relatable, everyone knows a coffee nerd
6. **Visual identity opportunity** — "Craft Coffee Bag Label" is warm, distinctive, and nothing like existing apps in the log
7. **No overlap** with any previously built app in the style log
