## SceneSong
**Describe a moment. Get your soundtrack.**

### What Was Built
SceneSong is an AI music prompt generator that turns any life scene or feeling into tailored prompts for the three leading AI music platforms: Google Lyria 3, Suno, and Udio.

The app features a keyword-scoring analysis engine that processes natural language scene descriptions across 6 dimensions:
- **Energy** (still → electric)
- **Warmth** (cold/clinical → golden/intimate)
- **Complexity** (sparse/solo → layered/orchestral)
- **Tempo Feel** (glacial → urgent)
- **Social Space** (deeply solitary → communal/crowded)
- **Light/Dark Valence** (melancholy → hopeful)

From these scores, the engine derives: key/mode, tempo range, lead instrumentation, mood tags, and a 5-color emotional palette — then generates three distinct, platform-formatted prompts.

### Why This
Google Lyria 3 launched in Gemini just 2 days ago (Feb 18, 2026) and went viral immediately — the most talked-about AI launch of the week. It lets users generate 30-second music from text or image prompts. The natural companion app: a tool that helps you write *better* prompts. SceneSong fills that gap by making the emotional/musical translation intuitive — you describe a *feeling*, not a genre, and the engine handles the musical vocabulary.

### Visual Style Direction
**"Analog Warmth / Cassette Punk"**

The antithesis of dark glassmorphism. Deep warm blacks and rich browns (#0E0804, #2C1810) with amber accents (#E8A050) instead of blues or purples. The visual metaphor is a recording studio — tactile, physical, warm-glowing.

**Signature Element:** A 6-channel VU meter array that animates with staggered delays as the scene processes — each channel filling like real analog signal meters, with color gradations (green → yellow → red for energy, blue → amber for warmth, etc.). The UI is housed in a cassette-tape frame with corner reel circles.

Typography: Space Mono (technical/monospace throughout) + Oswald (headers, labels) — gives it a retrofuturist recording equipment feel.
